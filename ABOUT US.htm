THE LIFE AND MINISTRY OFBISHOP ABERI K. BALYA.

Bishop Balya believed to have been born in 1877 in Rwahunga Village Kyaka County Kyenjojo District. His father was Kebisingo, Munyankole by tribe and Muliisa by clan. His mother was a Muhindakati -a princess born in Ankole kingdom Palace. Bishop Balya’s ancestors originated from Kyabukuju, Rubare, Ntungamo district.  Because of Bishop Balya’s good personality. King Kasagama of Tooro appointed him as head of the Kingdom Treasury store, servant and guards.

He was baptized and confirmed in 1901. He participated in the translation of Runyoro Rutooro Bible in 1910.

The Rt. Rev. Aberi Balya was consecrated and enthroned as the first Anglican Bishop in East and Central Africa on 26th October 1947 as Bishop of Uganda.

He held many positions of responsibilities and was given many national and international awards for his exemplary and dedicated services in the society and in the church. He retired in 1960 at the age of 83.

He died on 26th November 1979 at the age of 102. He was accorded a state burial on 30th November 1979 at St. John’s cathedral –Kabarole District. He is survived by a widow –Asta, four children and many grand children. There are institutions, dormitories, halls, and a road named after him.

                                                       MEMORIAL CHURCH.

Bishop Balya built a church near his home. Christians of that church, friends and well wishers have built and furnished a bigger church which was officially opened by Bishop of Ruwenzori Diocese on 28th October 2007. On that day of opening the church, activities were launched to commemorate 60 years since the consecration and the enthronement of Rt. Rev. Aberi Balya as first African Anglican Bishop in East and Central Africa. The church that Bishop Balya built is now the headquarters of what has been named as Bishop Balya Parish.
