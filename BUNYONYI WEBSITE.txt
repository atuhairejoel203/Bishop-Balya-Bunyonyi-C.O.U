<h1>BUNYONYi C.O.U</H1>
<p>Bunyonyi c.o.u is located in Kabarole district,Burahya county, Kibasi town council,300M from Kiijura main road.</p>
<p>It is under the dioses of Rwenzori, Kabarole Archdeconary,Kiburara zone.</p>
<p>It was found by Right Reverend Bishop Aberi Balya.He was conviced by the word of God in his way of preaching the gospel of salvation.He was the first Bishop in the north and south of sahara.</p>
<p>He gave out the biggest masses of land to the church, and he encouraged education by starting up  a primary school Bunyonyi primary school.It is now known of producing well educated and first class children of the nation .</p>
<p>Bunyonyi C.O.U was started in 1914 by Bishop Balya who was then a lay reader. By then,Bunyonyi was not yet a parish. It was under Kiburara C.O.U which was a parish.</p>
<p>Bishop Balya died in 1979 at the age of 103.</p>
